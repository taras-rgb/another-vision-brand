# Another Vision: image prompts

For Midjourney (or Firefly, or Runway). Generate 4 or more variations each and keep the best 1–2. Drop the finals into `Brand References/generated/`.

**Shared style:** paste this at the end of every prompt.

> shot on 35mm film, Kodak Portra 400, soft natural grain, hazy atmosphere, cool blue shadows and warm ember-orange highlights, shallow depth of field, calm editorial mood, generous empty space, no text, no logos --style raw

---

**1 · Architectural detail** (hero, social) `--ar 4:5`
Extreme close-up of the corner of a minimal white concrete villa against a deep blue dusk sky, last warm sunlight grazing one wall, crisp edge, clean geometry, most of the frame empty sky.

**2 · The view** (person, story) `--ar 4:5`
Back view of a person in a linen shirt standing on a villa terrace at golden hour looking out to a hazy ocean horizon, backlit, rim light on hair and shoulders, figure small in the lower third.

**3 · Eye macro** (the "vision" image, markers on top) `--ar 4:5`
Extreme macro of a human eye at golden hour, warm amber skin tones, the iris reflecting a modern villa with a glowing window, eyelashes catching light, very shallow focus.

**4 · Interior light** (website, case studies) `--ar 16:9`
Minimal living room with floor-to-ceiling glass, long beams of morning light crossing a pale stone floor, soft orange light on the walls, blue shade in the corners, no people, quiet.

**5 · Blur field** (backgrounds for frosted cards) `--ar 16:9`
Completely out-of-focus coastline at sunset, large soft bokeh shapes of blue sky and orange sun, no recognisable objects, smooth gradients, painterly.

**6 · Hand and device** (product, social) `--ar 4:5`
A hand holding a smartphone showing a property website with a villa photo, on a terrace at golden hour, the screen sharp and the background softly blurred, warm skin, blue evening light behind.

**7 · Aerial geometry** (case study covers) `--ar 1:1`
Top-down aerial of a modern villa roof and a long rectangular pool at dusk, sharp graphic geometry, turquoise water, warm light along one edge, surrounding greenery in shadow.

---

**Tips**
- Use `--sref` with the Hematonix reference image (or your favourite) to lock the colour mood across all seven.
- If skin or hands look artificial, regenerate. Weak images were the main problem last round.
